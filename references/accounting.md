# Accounting and proof limits

Money stays as BigInt internally and decimal strings in JSON. Quotes and order-reported output are estimates/reports, never automatic proof of native net receipt. Gas comes from canonical receipt gasUsed × effectiveGasPrice. ERC20 actual spends/receipts use wallet net Transfer logs; reverted or noncanonical receipts do not enter the ledger.

Funding cost includes actual BNB principal plus funding Gas. Buys consume payment-coin basis proportionally and include buy/approval Gas. The unused payment coin retains its exact remaining basis. `accounts` rebuilds confirmed funding, buys, share sales, and explicit exits in chain order. Share sale into quote coins transfers basis plus sale Gas into the quote inventory; quoted BNB value is not realized profit. Partial source spend consumes only that portion of basis; unknown/external inventory makes basis unknown.

`portfolio --json` includes active `rows`, separate `historyRows`, funding balances, cash settlements and accounts. Only successfully observed zero share AND zero actual-token balances move to history; unreadable markets remain active with errors. Historical buy cost and realized proceeds remain distinct from zero remaining cost. Saved/offline snapshots preserve both sections, including older snapshot migration. `accounts` saves accounts-latest.json.

`reconcile-history` performs read-only canonical evidence checks and reports recoverable funding chronology and purchase basis. Add `--apply` to atomically save verified repairs to the local ledger (never a wallet mutation). The command binds sender, calldata, block/hash/index, confirmations, exact token transfers, frozen inventory, finite approval Gas and historical balance anchors. Wallet quote-token transfer coverage excludes unregistered external movements, including net-neutral changes. Partial exits consume basis proportionally even when actual native proceeds remain unknown. Missing archival states/logs, missing Gas proofs or incomplete inventory origins preserve unknown costs and provide warnings. This is bounded evidence reconciliation, not complete wallet-history discovery. It does not guess source cost from today's price or treat an absent approval Gas field as zero.

## Estimated exit Gas

Native internal-sale Gas uses pinned `eth_call` and `eth_estimateGas`. ERC20 internal exits add an estimated payment-token-to-BNB leg; already received launched tokens use that estimate alone. No sell, approval, signing or wallet preview is executed to estimate it.

Because the official quote JSON omits Gas and executable route calldata, the fallback uses successful canonical wallet token-call receipts from recorded funding/exits within seven days. Prefer the same token, otherwise the largest observed Gas usage; reprice at current RPC Gas price and add 25%. Include separately simulated reset plus finite approval to the sample contract, also with 25% provision. This deliberately provisions approvals even when the future route may reuse allowance. JSON includes sample hashes, sample contract, same-asset flag, price, margin and pinned block. These are historical-sample estimates; future routes and actual Gas may differ and the estimate is not a maximum fee. No samples, stale/invalid evidence or failed approval simulation means unknown Gas and unknown full net PnL. Quotes already include their trading fee treatment; Gas is deducted once and missing fees are never assumed zero.

`reconcile EVIDENCE_JSON` is an offline evidence-calculation tool, not a trusted import or an instruction to modify balances. It accepts evidence already verified by an independent canonical RPC/trace check. `receipt.canonical=true` is an assertion by the evidence producer; this command alone does not establish it. Verified before/after BNB objects must identify wallet, block/hash/parentHash and raw balance, with no other wallet balance movement. Plain balances or reported order output alone leave actual native output unknown. Do not mark evidence verified without checking its source. The pure functions are documented in src/settlement.mjs and tested with synthetic proofs.

Current limits: this version does not obtain complete native internal-call traces or index all external wallet history. Native sale/DEX-exit realized BNB PnL stays unknown when complete payout evidence is unavailable. This is deliberate rather than counting gross reported amounts as net. Costs, refunds and remaining quote quantities can still be reconciled exactly when source evidence is complete. No automatic dust cleanup, claim or historical personal-ledger migration.
